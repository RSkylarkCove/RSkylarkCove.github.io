Tick Tock Blocks

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Tick Tock Blocks (“the app”) is made by RSkylark Cove (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the app on Android and iOS.

Summary

  • You can play Classic, Rush and Versus against the computer without an account. Your scores and settings then stay on your device.
  • If you sign in, your ranking name, scores and Versus stars appear in the rankings, and you can add friends and play live matches.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID.
  • We never sell your personal information. You can delete your account at any time.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and app interaction data to show ads and measure them. On iPhone, you are asked for permission before your activity is used for tracking. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can remove banners and full-screen ads with the one-time “Remove Ads” purchase. Reward videos are always optional.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database): accounts, rankings, friends, live matches and support messages. Data is stored on Google Cloud servers. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob: ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing and Apple App Store: in-app purchases
  • Sign in with Google (and, where offered, Sign in with Apple): only if you choose them. We receive your name, email and an account ID; we never post anything on your behalf or access your contacts

How long we keep information

Account data, your friends list and ranking entries are kept while your account exists. Rankings for past days and months may be kept so that rankings and rewards stay accurate, but they show only your ranking name. Live match data is only needed during the match. Support messages are kept as long as needed to help you. When you delete your account, your account, friends list and current ranking entries are deleted.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: ⚙️ Settings → Delete account → Delete forever.

Without the app: email us at rskylarkcove-support@googlegroups.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my Tick Tock Blocks account”. We will delete your account and related data within 30 days.

Deleting your account also deletes the token balance and Remove Ads record saved to it. Purchases stay linked to your Google Play or App Store account and can be restored with “Restore purchases”. Data saved only on your device is removed by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Security rules make sure players can only change their own entries, and support messages can only be read by our support team.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, the GDPR, or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to its use. Contact us to make a request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
