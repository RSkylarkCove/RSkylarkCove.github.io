Tick Tock Blocks

Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Tick Tock Blocks account (by RSkylark Cove) and the data linked to it.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open Tick Tock Blocks and tap ⚙️ Settings.
  2. In your account box, tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete forever.

Option 2 — By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email rskylarkcove-support@googlegroups.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my Tick Tock Blocks account”. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email address and account ID)
  • Your profile, friend code, friends list and friend requests
  • The progress, Replace token balance and Remove Ads record saved to your account (these can’t be restored after deletion)
  • Your current entries in the Classic, Rush and Versus rankings

What is kept

  • Your Google Play or Apple payment receipts, which those stores keep under their own policies.
  • Scores, tokens and settings saved on your phone stay until you uninstall the app.
  • Support messages you sent are kept only as long as needed to finish helping you.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob for ads is handled by Google under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
