Owl Grove: Logic Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Owl Grove: Logic Puzzle (“the app”) is made by RSkylark Cove (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the app on Android and iOS.

Summary

  • You can play without an account. Your progress then stays on your device.
  • If you sign in, we store your game progress so you can continue on another device, and your chosen name appears in the rankings.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID.
  • We never sell your personal information. You can delete your account at any time.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and app interaction data to show ads and measure them. On iPhone, you are asked for permission before your activity is used for tracking. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can remove full-screen ads with the one-time “Remove Ads” purchase. Reward videos are always optional.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): accounts, cloud save and rankings. Data is stored on Google Cloud servers. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob: ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing and Apple App Store: in-app purchases
  • Sign in with Google, and, where offered, Facebook Login and Sign in with Apple: only if you choose them. We receive your name, email and an account ID; we never post anything on your behalf or access your contacts. With “Hide My Email”, we only receive a private relay address

How long we keep information

Account data and cloud saves are kept while your account exists. Ranking entries for past days, weeks and months may be kept so that rankings and rewards stay accurate, but they show only your ranking name. When you delete your account, your account and cloud save are deleted.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and cloud save.

Without the app: email us at rskylarkcove-support@googlegroups.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my Owl Grove account”. We will delete your account, cloud save and ranking entries within 30 days.

Purchases stay linked to your Google Play or App Store account and can be restored with “Restore purchase”. Progress saved only on your device is removed by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Cloud saves can only be read or changed by the signed-in account they belong to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, the GDPR, or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to its use. Contact us to make a request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
