NoxPatch: Cozy Logic Puzzle

Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your NoxPatch: Cozy Logic Puzzle account (by RSkylark Cove) and the data linked to it.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open NoxPatch and tap ⚙️ Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete forever.

Option 2 — By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email rskylarkcove-support@googlegroups.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my NoxPatch account”. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email address and account ID)
  • Your cloud save (levels, hints, settings and rewards)
  • Your entries in the Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Top Level rankings

What is kept

  • Purchases stay linked to your Google Play or Apple account, so you can restore them if you play again.
  • Progress saved only on your phone stays until you uninstall the app.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob for ads is handled by Google under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
